Concept

A network of live performers, not just another DJ roster.

CTRLtek Live brings together artists who can turn their electronic production into performance: clips, instruments, sequences, effects, machines, and improvisation are handled in real time.

The project is open to France and the international scene. Selection is based on artistic identity, performance level, live mastery, and the desire to collaborate — not on geographic location.

01

Live first

Ableton Live as the central environment, with MIDI controllers, instruments, and/or hybrid hardware.

02

Strong identity

Underground techno and electronic music: dark, hypnotic, industrial, melodic, warehouse-oriented, and experimental.

03

Real collaboration

Jams, shared live sets, remixes, compilations, showcases, workflow exchanges, and booking opportunities.

Founder

IssamFire

IssamFire is a producer, DJ, and live performer based in Toulouse, France. His universe explores melodic, dark, industrial, and hypnotic sides of techno, between cinematic tension, warehouse energy, and progressive groove construction.

His catalog includes releases on G-Mafia Records and BeatCode. On stage and in the studio, his approach is built around Ableton Live, Maschine, MIDI controllers, and machines, with a performance mindset rather than simple track playback.

With CTRLtek Live, he is developing a shared framework to connect compatible live artists, create collaborations, and gradually build a stage identity able to move through France and the international scene.

Join CTRLtek Live

Open to live electronic artists worldwide.

We are primarily looking for producers and live performers with a strong identity, a real performance setup, and the desire to build collaborations. Ableton Live is the common language of the project; MIDI controllers and hybrid setups make it possible to manipulate the set in real time.

Essential criteria

  • Ableton Live as the central performance environment
  • MIDI controller and/or hybrid hardware setup manipulated live
  • Ability to deliver a true live performance — not only a DJ set
  • Identity compatible with techno and underground electronic music
  • Willingness to collaborate, share, and build common projects
